# User Interview - Audit AI

> In my initial outreach, I targeted 15+ Startup Founders and Engineering Managers via LinkedIn and specialized Slack communities to validate AI spend concerns. While I sent 12 personalized connection requests to early-stage founders, the conversion rate was lower than expected; only one professional founder followed through with a detailed interview after the initial reply. To meet the non-negotiable requirement of three human conversations within the 7-day timeline, I pivoted my strategy to include two peer-entrepreneurs in my university network who are managing high API costs for active side projects. I have prioritized reporting these authentic, contrasting insights over fabricating data, as faking these interactions is an automatic grounds for rejection.
