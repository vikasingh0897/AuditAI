The exact target user is a **Seed-stage CTO or Founding Engineer** at a company with 5–20 employees. These users are technically savvy enough to manage their own stack but too busy to audit individual seat costs monthly. They are currently "bleeding cash" on unoptimized seats and are the primary decision-makers for infrastructure pivots .
