# METRICS.md

### 🌟 North Star Metric

**Total Identified Annual Savings (TIAS):** The sum of all potential annual savings surfaced to users.

- **Why:** This directly measures the "Value Realized" by the user. If this number is high, the tool is doing its job as a "lead-gen magnet" for Credex .

---

### 🔢 Input Metrics

1.  **Form Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who land on the page and complete the 6-step tool input.
2.  **Public URL Shares:** Number of unique shareable URLs generated vs. total audits.
3.  **High-Savings Lead Quality:** Percentage of audits that surface >$500/mo in savings.

---

### 🛠️ Instrumentation

We will instrument the **"Start Free Audit"** button and the **"Book Consultation"** click-throughs using basic event tracking (e.g., PostHog or Umami) to monitor the funnel without compromising user privacy.

---

### 🛑 Pivot Signal

We will consider a pivot if our **Consultation Booking Rate** for "High-Savings" users falls below **1%** over 500 audits. This would indicate that either the savings reasoning isn't "defensible" enough to drive action, or the target user doesn't trust the Credex credit model .
