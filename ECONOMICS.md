# ECONOMICS.md

### 💰 Lead Value to Credex

A converted lead is worth approximately **$2,500 - $5,000 ARR** to Credex in the first year. This is based on a conservative 10% margin on a $25k-$50k annual AI infrastructure spend (standard for a 10-person AI startup) .

---

### 📉 CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)

Since we are using organic channels (X, HN, and Viral Loops), our initial CAC is primarily **Opportunity Cost (Founder Time)**. As we scale, we estimate a Paid CAC of **$50 per booked consultation** through targeted LinkedIn ads aimed at "Engineering Managers" .

---

### 📊 Conversion Math for Profitability

To be profitable, we need the following funnel performance:

1.  **Audit Completed → Consultation Booked:** 5% conversion.
2.  **Consultation → Credit Purchase:** 20% conversion.
    Given these numbers, for every 1,000 audits, we generate 10 customers. At an average profit of $3,000 per customer, 1,000 audits generate **$30,000 in gross profit**.

---

### 🚀 The Path to $1M ARR

To drive $1M ARR in 18 months, the following must be true:

- The tool must process **~35,000 audits per year** .
- We must expand the "Audit Engine" to include high-spend categories like **AWS Bedrock and GCP Vertex AI** to increase the average deal size.
- Credex must maintain a retention rate of **>85%** for credit renewals.

_Note: All figures are rough estimates based on current AI market trends and the discounted credit model ._
